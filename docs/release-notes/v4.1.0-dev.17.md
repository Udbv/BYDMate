## BYDMate Waze 4.1.0-dev.17 (development pre-release)

### Added
- ADAS shadow mode (#23, Settings → Diagnostics, on by default). While the car is on, every 2 s
  the app builds the messages navigation would pass to the driver assistance from Waze's data:
  the navigation status, `TrafficInfo` (speed limit, camera, distance and time to the
  destination) and `naviActionAndCamera` (manoeuvre, camera), in the factory map's format.
  Nothing is sent: the messages are only logged (tag `AdasShadow`) when they change. Next to
  them, read only, the log shows what the car itself sees: the camera's sign, the speed-limit
  assist's limit and allowance, the cruise mode and speed, the lane assist state and NOA
  availability. Every minute and at the end of a drive: how often Waze's limit matched the
  camera's sign, where they differed (Waze/car pairs) and where only one knew it. Speed bumps and
  crossings are not built yet: Waze's alert banner has to be read first.
