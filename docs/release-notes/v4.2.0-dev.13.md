## BYDMate Waze 4.2.0-dev.13 (development pre-release)

### Added
- Ukrainian voices: Tetiana and Mykyta (one shared model, 23 MB) and Lada (14 MB). They are the
  offline piper uk_UA models from sherpa-onnx. Pick one where the Russian voices are, in the voice
  assistant settings.
- The BYDMate navigation voice speaks the language of the selected voice. With a Ukrainian voice
  it says «Через 400 метрів поверніть праворуч на …», «Зараз ліворуч», «На колі третій з'їзд».
  The assistant's other answers are still in Russian.
