## BYDMate Waze 4.1.0-dev.16 (development pre-release)

### Fixed
- A rule with pauses («Зимний старт») cut by switching the car off between steps finishes on
  the next start. The car keeps the heaters as they were while the app's process stops at car
  off: on 30.10 the car was switched off a minute after the start, the rule was in its first
  pause, and the seat at 3 and the wheel heater stayed on. Now the run's place is saved before
  each step; on the next start (within an hour) the run continues on its own clock: pauses that
  already ended are skipped and the heaters go straight to the step they would be at by now. A
  rule edited in the meantime, or fired again, is not resumed.
- The 12 V current (added in 4.0.1-dev.12) wrote a log line on every change, about once a
  second: 73 % of the automatic trip log. Like the other continuous readings it now writes one
  line a minute; the data snapshot still updates on every event.
