## BYDMate Waze 4.2.0-dev.4 (development pre-release)

### Added
- The car suggests speed bumps itself (#22). The first background recording (3.11, 20 minutes of
  city) showed the DiSus suspension sends no events on this car, while the ADAS body pitch rate
  comes 25–40 times a second: ordinary roughness swings it ±5–6 °/s, hard double hits ±9–15 °/s.
  The detection runs in the daemon, where every sample is seen (the app's push keeps one per
  200 ms and loses the peaks): a pitch swing of 22 °/s peak to peak within 0.6 s, at 5–70 km/h,
  not under hard braking, once per 8 s (the rear axle's hit is the same bump). On the 3.11
  recording that is 4 suggestions in 20 minutes. The app picks them up every 3 seconds, asks
  «Лежачий?» and opens the microphone for 8 seconds by itself: «да / так» saves the point where
  the bump was felt (the fix of that moment, not of the answer), «нет / ні» saves nothing. At most
  once per 30 seconds and never over the driver's own voice session. Every suggestion and answer
  goes to the trip log (`BUMP …` with the swing and the speed), and swings from 14 °/s are noted in
  the recorder's file: the threshold is tuned on the answers.
