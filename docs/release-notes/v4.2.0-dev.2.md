## BYDMate Waze 4.2.0-dev.2 (development pre-release)

### Added
- The trip under way on the home screen (#42): while a drive goes on it is the first row of the
  trip list, in the same columns as finished trips (start, time so far, km, kWh, kWh/100), edged
  in green, with «now», the energy won back and the outside temperature under it. Km and
  consumption are the widget's; regeneration comes from the per-minute data, charging minutes
  left out. Refreshed every 30 seconds; the row appears after the first 100 m and gives way to
  the finished trip once it is recorded.
- An app update in the middle of a drive shows in the trip log (#44): the log header reads «app
  update» when the app was updated in the last 3 minutes, and with a trip open a `TRIP open trip
  at restart` line tells how long the gap was, how far the trip had gone and what became of it
  (continued, closed, left to the energy data source).
- The full trip log goes out in 20-minute parts (#43): every 20 minutes the file is closed, sent
  to Telegram and the recording goes on in a new one, so a long drive in progress can be read
  without the Send button. The file used to end only after 2 hours, and then was never sent: the
  next recording overwrote the record of it. A file that did not go out for any reason is now
  sent when the next one starts.
- ADAS shadow mode counts who knew a speed limit first, Waze or the car's camera (#23). The car
  keeps the last sign until the next, and entering a town (50) or leaving it (90) is marked by a
  name board the camera does not read as a limit, while Waze has it from its map. Every stretch
  where the two differ is an episode: «Waze first» (Waze changed, the camera came to the same
  value later: the km and seconds the car drove on a stale limit), «camera first» (a sign Waze
  did not know yet), and the rest. One line per episode and a drive summary (km with the limits
  agreeing and differing, episodes by kind) go to the trip log. Distance comes from the speed;
  no positions are written.

### Fixed
- Parked ADAS test: a run cut short by leaving the settings screen ended with «SOME/IP gateway
  unavailable» although every frame had gone out (31.10 17:03). It now ends «interrupted»;
  «gateway unavailable» only when the gateway really cannot be reached. The clean-up still runs
  however the test ends.
