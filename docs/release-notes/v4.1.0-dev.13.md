## BYDMate Waze 4.1.0-dev.13 (development pre-release)

### Changed
- Upstream AndyShaman/BYDMate 4.0.2, 4.0.3 and 4.1.0 merged in:
  - Tech: the «Car state» card (tyres on a car drawing, climate, PM2.5) and the «Diagnostics» card
    (cluster warnings, days and km to service, a fault-code scan of the car's modules, on P and on
    tap only, read-only, codes with descriptions from BYD manuals, sent to Telegram on request);
  - rear seat heating in automation where the car reports it;
  - trips: two short drives minutes apart stay two trips, trips are recorded again after a head
    unit reset, a future-dated record no longer stops the import;
  - driver seat heat level 3 on cars with the fallback channel; the service no longer restarts on a
    parked car; voice keeps the start of a phrase on Android 10 and is audible on external-amp
    cars; split-screen steering key; trip time, kWh and cost in the Telegram report.
- The fork's own work stays: trip page, cluster power card, stepped «Зимний старт», ambient at
  night, full trip log.
- Database version 25: the fork's per-minute table and upstream's tombstone start time. A
  4.0.1-dev.12 database (the fork's version 24) moves over without loss.
- Ukrainian texts for all of upstream's new screens.
