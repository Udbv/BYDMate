## BYDMate Waze 4.1.0-dev.21 (development pre-release)

### Fixed
- Waze lanes on the HUD. On three drives (30–31.10) not one lane was recognised: the app's
  table only knows the big manoeuvre arrows, Waze's thin lane arrows matched none of them, and a
  lane without a recognised arrow is not sent to the HUD. An arrow the table does not know is now
  read by its shape: where the stem stands at the bottom, whether the head sits right over it
  (straight) and whether a branch reaches out left or right in the upper half (a turn),
  including straight-and-right in one lane. The recommended lane is still the brightest, its
  direction the manoeuvre's. A crop whose arrow does not reach the bottom of its cell (Waze's
  strip still sliding in, as most of those frames were) and a U-turn hook are not read: an empty
  HUD beats a wrong arrow.
