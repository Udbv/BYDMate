## BYDMate Waze 4.1.0-dev.14 (development pre-release)

### Added
- Trip weather: every minute of a drive now keeps whether the wipers ran and the outside
  temperature. A drive is rainy when the wipers ran in at least a third of its minutes, snowy when
  that happened at +1 °C or colder. The trips list shows the temperature and ☂ or ❄ before the
  time; the trip page gets a «Conditions» line (temperature, weather, city / mixed / highway).
  Drives from before this version show the temperature from their start and end, weather unknown.
- «Forecast vs actual» on the trip page: the expected consumption from earlier drives in similar
  conditions (10 °C temperature bands, speed class under 40 / 40–70 / over 70 km/h, weather) next
  to the real one, the difference in percent and how many drives the forecast stands on. A narrow
  group with few drives leans on the wider one, so two drives cannot swing it. Each drive's
  forecast learns only from the drives before it; the accuracy line shows the mean error and bias
  over the last 30 drives next to the error of a plain average, to see how much the conditions
  help and where to tune. Drives under 3 km are left out.
- Elevation in the forecast: a drive's start and end height come from its GPS track (the mean of
  the first and last 30 s). The model learns the flat consumption (actual minus the energy of the
  climb, plus what regen returned on the way down) and the forecast adds the drive's own height
  change: 100 m up costs about 0.8 kWh. Under 10 m is GPS noise. «Conditions» shows the height
  change in metres, the expected line its share in kWh.
- Database version 26: two new per-minute fields (wipers, outside temperature).
