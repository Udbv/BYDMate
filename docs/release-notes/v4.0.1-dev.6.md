## BYDMate Waze 4.0.1-dev.6 (development pre-release)

### Added
- «Потужність на приборці» (#5), Settings → Display, off by default: a card on the instrument cluster
  with the battery power (regeneration in green), the front/rear motor split in kW and percent and a
  2-minute graph. Shown the whole time the car is on, in place of the stock right card; position and
  size are set with sliders. It uses the cluster projection display like the blind-spot cameras; not
  yet tried on a Tang L, every step is logged under `ClusterPowerCard`.
- Notes on the Tang L sensor recording of 2026-10-25: data BYDMate does not use yet, and whether the
  car can be made calmer and which cruise speed is cheapest (`docs/investigations/`).
