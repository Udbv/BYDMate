## BYDMate Waze 3.15.0

First stable release of the Waze fork, cut after the feature was verified in the car
(BYD Tang L EV 2025, DiLink 150). Includes upstream BYDMate through 3.14.3.

Installs beside the official app as **BYDMate Waze** (`com.bydmate.app.waze`) and updates itself
from this repository. Settings -> Updates chooses the Stable or Development channel.

### New
- **Waze guidance on the factory head-up display**, next to Yandex Navigator and Yandex Maps:
  maneuver, distance, street, remaining route and time, speed limit. When Waze draws the arrow as
  an image with no text, it is classified from a bounded screenshot of the arrow view.
- **HUD type** (Settings -> Display -> HUD): Auto (from `ro.vehicle.type`), Classic (Atto 3, Seal,
  Sea Lion) and AR-HUD (DiLink 150, Tang L). On the Tang L the glass is driven through the
  instrument-panel features, which is on by default; the SOME/IP HUD frame and the factory-map
  context remain as switches and are off.
- **Navigator language packs** in `assets/navi/phrases/`: English, Russian, Czech, Chinese and
  Ukrainian. Maneuver phrases, units and time formats live in the language file, so adding a
  language is one file and no code.
- **Ukrainian interface** (Settings -> Language), all 1237 strings.
- **Auto-close of the "This car is not from official export" window** (Settings -> Display), off by
  default. Verified in the car.

### Fixed
- The arrow no longer disappears while the car is stopped: the Waze window is re-read after 20 s of
  silence while guidance is active.
- Turning the projection off, or changing the HUD type, now resets the instrument features, so the
  last arrow no longer stays on the glass.
- Info-level field diagnostics survive the release build: HUD frames, Waze reads, the resolved HUD
  type and the opened service keys.

### Firmware investigations
`docs/investigations/` documents the HUD and SOME/IP contract on the Tang L, why NOA is
unavailable without Chinese map data, the export-warning window, and how the car's Amap engine
differs from the mobile app.

### Known limitations
- Road name and lane guidance on the glass are not implemented yet; both need a byte-array and an
  array feature write that the helper daemon does not offer.
- The SOME/IP HUD frame does nothing on the Tang L; it is kept for other DiLink 150 trims.
