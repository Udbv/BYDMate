## BYDMate Waze 4.2.0-dev.9 (development pre-release)

### Added
- Home Assistant (#26). Settings → Integrations → «Home Assistant» registers the car in HA as a
  Mobile App device (https address and a long-lived token, «Перевірити» button). While the car
  is on, it updates charge, range, odometer, temperatures, doors, windows, lock and tyre
  pressures once a minute. The token goes only to the HA API; webhook calls carry none.
  `docs/home-assistant/` has a ready HA package and a guide for the BYDMate webhook.
- Reviewing marked points (#22). The point card on the map has «Підтвердити» / «Відхилити», and
  the pin colour shows the status. A rejected point goes on a blacklist: the car no longer
  suggests a speed bump at that spot. A WME userscript (`tools/wme/`) shows the exported points
  as a layer for map editors.
- Reserve at arrival (#6). While Waze guides a route, the floating widget shows
  «На прибытии: ~X км запаса» or «Не хватит ~Y км», coloured like the widget border. The
  distance is tied to its own route and its age. On by default; the switch is in the widget
  settings.
- Audio channel test (#6). Settings → Service & data → Diagnostics plays a phrase on the media,
  navigation, assistant and notification channels in turn, once ducking the music and once
  without. The screen and the log show what the audio system answered to each variant.
- Cruise advice (#23, safe class). With adaptive cruise on and the Waze limit at least 5 km/h
  below the set speed, the voice says «Ограничение 50, круиз 70». Nothing is sent to the car.
  Off by default.
