## BYDMate Waze 4.2.0-dev.23 (development pre-release)

### Fixed
- Trips were being lost: about an hour of driving on the morning of 14.11, and probably ~9 more trips on 11-13.11. The detector for BYD's "dead" energy file cleared its flag on any file change (e.g. overnight charging), after which the app stopped recording trips on its own. The flag is now cleared only when a real trip appears in the file.
- The copy of the BYD file is more reliable, and import errors are now written to the log.
- Warm-up no longer merges into the trip: the trip starts at the first movement.

### Added
- The app always keeps its own safety-net trip and saves it when BYD recorded none (no duplicates).
- Anomaly detector: at start and shutdown it compares odometer and charge with the recorded trips; more than 1 km without a trip produces a recovered trip (source `recovered`) with a GPS route and a ♻️ line in the Telegram report.
- The first pass after the update looks 14 days back and restores trips that were already lost.

### What to check on the car
1. After a drive, the trip appears in history even if BYD did not record its own.
2. Recovered trips (if any) show up once, with no duplicates of real trips.
