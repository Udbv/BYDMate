## BYDMate Waze 4.1.0-dev.15 (development pre-release)

### Changed
- The consumption forecast learns minute by minute instead of per drive: each minute keeps its
  own outside temperature, weather (wipers within two minutes), speed and height change. A cold
  morning start that warms up on the way, or a mountain pass, counts where it happened instead of
  as one average for the whole drive. The minutes' energy and distance only set the shares and
  are scaled to the car's counters for the drive; minutes that disagree with them by more than a
  factor of two leave the drive as one piece.
- Height comes from the whole track: each minute's height is smoothed with its neighbours, climbs
  cost energy, descents give part of it back through regen. A pass up and down no longer cancels
  out as start minus finish.
- A small group of conditions leans on the wider one by distance driven (30 km weight) instead of
  by number of drives.
- The trip page's «Conditions» show the temperature range («−3…+5 °C») and the height gained and
  lost («up +420 m, down −380 m»).
