## BYDMate Waze 4.1.0-dev.22 (development pre-release)

### Fixed
- Waze lanes on the HUD, the real cause. Near a junction Waze puts its lane strip where the
  manoeuvre arrow was and the arrow node goes away. The app aimed every screenshot at the arrow,
  so in the 31.10 logs (dev.18 and dev.21) the screenshots stopped the very second the lanes came
  up, and lanes were only caught in the transition frames that still had both (hence the
  half-drawn arrows). With no arrow but a lane strip on screen, the screenshot now targets the
  strip.
- The HUD arrow no longer drops to «straight» while Waze shows lanes instead of the arrow: the
  arrow read just before is kept (31.10: a right turn shown as straight for the whole approach).
- A screenshot the system never answered (the navigator window went away under it) can no longer
  stop arrow reading for the rest of the drive: after 10 s it counts as lost.
- The trip log says why a screenshot gave nothing (`VISUAL no read: …`, only when the reason
  changes).
