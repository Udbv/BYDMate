## BYDMate Waze 4.0.1-dev.4 (development pre-release)

### Added
- A sensor recording the car was powered off on (so the upload on stop never ran) goes to the bot by
  itself at the next service start: zipped, once, after a minute for the modem, retried on network
  errors. A recording the daemon is still writing is left alone.

### Fixed
- The «motors» section of the diagnostic log reads the front torque, rear torque F, indicated and
  target torque as floats: read as ints on the Tang L they answered -10013.
- `tools/tg-logs/fetch.py` waits up to 20 s for Telegram to show the next pinned file after an unpin;
  it used to stop after the first one.
