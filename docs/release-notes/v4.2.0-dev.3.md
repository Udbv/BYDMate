## BYDMate Waze 4.2.0-dev.3 (development pre-release)

### Added
- Background recording for speed bumps (#22, Settings → Diagnostics, on by default in
  development builds): every drive the sensor recorder records by itself only what a bump shows
  in — the DiSus suspension's vertical accelerations, body pitch and accelerations (ADAS), speed
  and wheels — at the car's own rate in windows around every body pitch or suspension event.
  Every 20 minutes a part is zipped to the Telegram bot and deleted from the car once sent; the
  last part goes a minute after power-off. A recording started by hand is left alone. The bump
  detection that will suggest a point and ask for a voice confirmation is tuned on these
  recordings of ordinary drives.
- «My points» for Waze (#22, first step). By voice, offline: «лежачий тут», «светофор тут»,
  «полосы тут», «отметь точку» (and the Ukrainian «лежачий поліцейський», «світлофор», «смуги»,
  «позначь»). The car's position is saved with its course and the voice says what was marked.
  The same place within 15 m on the same course is not duplicated, its pass count goes up (there
  and back are two points: Waze's hazards have a direction). Without a precise GPS fix (newer than
  10 s, better than 25 m) nothing is saved and the voice says so.
- The map page shows the points (a tap opens a card: type, course, passes, date, Delete) and
  Export: the owner's Telegram bot gets a list for the Ukrainian Waze editor community, each
  point with a link opening Waze Map Editor right there, and a GeoJSON file. Only places and
  courses go out: no tracks, no trip times.
- Database version 28: the `road_points` table; points are backed up with the trips.
