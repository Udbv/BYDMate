## BYDMate Waze 4.2.0-dev.6 (development pre-release)

### Added
- Waze limits to ADAS while driving (#23; the owner's decision of 3.11: always, limit and cameras,
  a settings switch and a voice one). The car's camera reads only signs: it misses a town's entry
  (50) and exit (90) and keeps the last sign until the next one, while Waze has them from its map.
  The parked test showed the car takes the sent limit both as the sign and as the speed limit
  assist's limit, so with adaptive cruise on the car follows it as it follows a sign.
  Every 0.5 s while the car is on and the switch is on: with a Waze limit, "navigating", the limit
  and the camera ahead (when Waze shows one) go out, no manoeuvres. Without a Waze limit (none on
  the road, or the reading is older than 30 s), with the switch off or the car off, "stopped" and
  empty frames go out once and the car is back on its own camera.
  Settings → ADAS → «Обмеження Waze в ADAS», off by default; by voice «увімкни ліміти» /
  «вимкни ліміти» (also Russian «включи / выключи ограничения»), answered by voice. Every change of
  what is sent goes to the trip log (`ADAS live …`). The parked test pauses the sending while it runs.
