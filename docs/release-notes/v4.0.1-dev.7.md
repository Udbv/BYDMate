## BYDMate Waze 4.0.1-dev.7 (development pre-release)

### Added
- Diagnostic log section `--- pedal ---` (read only): whether the car has e-pedal (one-pedal driving),
  regeneration levels, power-response, learner and rapid-acceleration limits, Custom drive mode and auto
  hold, and their current values. First step towards a conservative one-pedal city mode.
- `docs/investigations/tang-l-one-pedal-city.md`: how the Tang L slows down when the pedal is released
  (regeneration fades below ~25 km/h), the city pedal map and a target for a gentle one-pedal mode.

### Changed
- The efficiency notes use the real vehicle mass (2882 kg kerb plus driver) and have a section on
  cutting consumption at 110–130 km/h.
