## BYDMate Waze 4.1.0-dev.18 (development pre-release)

### Added
- «Map» page (new tab): the car on an OpenStreetMap map with two reach rings from the current
  range. The outer one is the whole range on roads (range ÷ 1.25: a road is a quarter longer than
  the straight line on average), the inner one keeps a 15 % reserve. Search for an address or a
  place (OpenStreetMap Nominatim, nearby results first; needs internet, without it a «Search in
  Waze» button). A long press on the map picks a point. A picked point shows its road distance
  (estimated) and a verdict: reachable with a reserve, tight, or not without charging; «Route in
  Waze» opens Waze navigating to it.
