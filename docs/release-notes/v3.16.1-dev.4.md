## BYDMate Waze 3.16.1-dev.4 (development pre-release)

The ambient light comes back on by itself in the dark, and the floating widget can show
instantaneous power the way a Tesla does. Neither has been tried in a Tang L yet.

### Added
- **Ambient light at night** (Settings → Display, off by default). The car drops the ambient
  light between trips; BYDMate turns it on once per power cycle — 15 s after power-on if it is
  already dark, or when it gets dark on the way. Darkness is the sun below the horizon from GPS,
  or the light sensor (level ≤ 2) without a fix. Switched off by hand, it stays off until the
  next power-on, also across a BYDMate restart mid-drive. A failed write is retried twice, 10 s
  apart. The command (`ambient_light_on`, dev=1023) is validated on Leopard 3 only; every attempt
  is an `AmbientNight` line in the log. (#4)
- **Power graph in the widget** (Settings → Widget). A row under the widget with the last two
  minutes of power: draw above zero in white, regen below in green, current kW on the left.
  Battery power (HV voltage × current) is the source, the whole-kW motor reading the fallback.
  About 1 Hz while driving; the slower parked poll restarts the graph. (#5)

### Fixed
- Ukrainian: five strings that had no translation (quiet notification, both blind-spot cameras
  on the main screen, cell voltage scale) and failed lint.
- Lint `WrongConstant` in the helper daemon (`createPackageContext` flags named, same value).
