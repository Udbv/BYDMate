## BYDMate Waze 4.0.1-dev.1 (development pre-release)

The fork catches up with upstream AndyShaman/BYDMate 3.15.0 → 4.0.1 and keeps everything of its own:
Waze as the HUD guidance source, the Tang L instrument-panel channel, the HUD type choice, the navigator
language packs, Ukrainian, ambient light at night and the widget's power graph. Not yet driven in a Tang L.

### From upstream
- DM-i / DM-o hybrids detected by themselves: fuel card, litres per trip, fuel price in tariffs (4.0.0).
  Nothing changes on an EV.
- HUD: "HUD check", three ways to the glass (1 frames only, the default; 2 adds the car's navigation status
  and CAN; 3 adds the LAUNCHER_MAP_CN family), ETA and remaining route, more maneuver phrases, the stale
  arrow cleared after a turn (3.19.x).
- Any player's track, Spotify included, on the instrument cluster's music card (3.19.7).
- Automation: new tab, up to 20 actions, sliders, test run, rule import/export, HUD and media actions,
  battery heating.
- Telegram reports and bot, configuration save, ADB restore after a reboot, Android 14 fixes.
- Front / rear motor power share in "Tech" (3.15.4).

### How it was combined
- HUD: the fork's HUD type (Auto / Classic / AR-HUD) and its Tang L channels stay; upstream's ways sit next
  to them, and way 1, the default, behaves as before.
- The navigator window is re-read by upstream's timer (every 5 s while guided without events) instead of
  the fork's own 20 s Waze keep-alive.
- A route with no updates ends after 90 s again, as upstream (the fork had 40 s): a navigator that hides
  its guidance widgets for a while mid-route (#199) must not end the guidance.
- Street-name transliteration for ways 2 and 3 is upstream's (with a Russian/Belarusian table); the fork's
  instrument-panel channel keeps its own for now (`HudPanelTextSanitizer`).
- The fork's helper transaction codes moved above upstream's (61–63), so the two never collide.
- CSV export removed, as upstream did. The trip log toggle moved to "Diagnostics".
- Offline voice understands Russian phrases only since upstream 3.18.0.
- Ukrainian translation extended to the strings of upstream's new features.
