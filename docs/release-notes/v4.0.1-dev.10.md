## BYDMate Waze 4.0.1-dev.10 (development pre-release)

### Changed
- «Повний лог кожної поїздки» starts 3 s after the car comes on instead of 45 s: the head unit's
  log buffer holds only seconds, so the startup (the ambient light at night, launch automations) was
  missing from every file. A button recording still pending from the last trip resumes first, as
  before.
- «Амбієнт у темряві» says in the recorded log why it did or did not turn the light on: switched
  off, power state, session, dark or not and by what (sun position or light sensor), already done
  for this power cycle. A line is written only when one of these changes.
- The range estimate stays within what this car has really done: the consumption behind it is
  held between the lowest and the highest average of 100 km stretches of the last 120 days of
  trips (at least four stretches; before that, as before). A few short winter trips or one gentle
  highway leg no longer show a full-charge range nobody has ever driven. The manual temperature
  table is unchanged.
- «Зимний старт» warms fast and then backs off instead of keeping the seat at level 2 for the whole
  drive: driver seat 3 and the steering wheel heat for the first minute, seat 2 to the third minute,
  seat 1 to the eighth, then the seat off; mirror heat as before. An existing rule is moved over
  once at the update, only while its actions are still the template's (seat 2 + mirrors); its
  conditions stay as set. Changing the heat by hand during those 8 minutes does not stop the steps.
