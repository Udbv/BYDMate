## BYDMate Waze 4.2.0-dev.12 (development pre-release)

### Added
- BYDMate navigation voice. On this car Waze's own voice interrupts the music instead of ducking
  it, and Waze cannot be patched. So BYDMate now voices Waze's manoeuvres itself, from the data it
  already reads.
- When it speaks:
  - at 1000 m, if the manoeuvre was first seen further than 1200 m away;
  - at 400 m;
  - "now", at 60 m.
- What it covers: turns, U-turns, the roundabout exit number, and arrival.
- Each phrase is said once per manoeuvre, at most once every 4 s.
- It speaks with the local voice. You choose the audio channel (navigation, assistant,
  notification or media) and whether music is ducked.
- Settings → «Дисплей». Off by default. Turn Waze's own voice off: Waze → Settings → Voice & sound.
- Every phrase goes to the trip log (`NAVVOICE`). Without a downloaded voice model it stays silent.
