## BYDMate Waze 4.0.1-dev.9 (development pre-release)

### Added
- «Повний лог кожної поїздки» (Settings → Diagnostics), on by default in development builds: the app
  log recording starts by itself once the car has been on for 45 s and ends a minute after it goes
  off; the file is saved to Downloads and, with a bot connected, sent to Telegram zipped. When the
  car cuts power first, the file is finished and sent at the next start instead of being resumed
  into the new trip. A stop from Settings keeps it off until the car goes off; a trip longer than the
  2 h limit continues in a new file.

### Changed
- The trip guidance log goes to Telegram after each trip by default in development builds.
- The recorded log now includes the massage keeper, the cluster power card, the HUD lane strip and
  the automatic trip log.
