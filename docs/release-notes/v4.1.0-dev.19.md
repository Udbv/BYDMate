## BYDMate Waze 4.1.0-dev.19 (development pre-release)

### Changed
- Map: a route instead of the reach rings. A picked point (search or long press) gets a road
  route at once (OSRM on the OpenStreetMap servers), its line coloured by three scenarios: green
  where the charge lasts even in the heavy case (the worst 100 km of the drive history: cold,
  highway), yellow in the usual case (the current range estimate), orange only when driving
  economically (the best 100 km of the history), red not without charging. Dots on the route
  mark where each scenario's charge runs out. The point's card shows the route's length and time
  and, per scenario, what is left at the end or how much is missing; the top right shows each
  scenario's range. The energy is what the car reports left in the battery.

- «Зимний старт» starts on the cabin temperature, not the outside one: the heat is for the
  people inside, and a cabin kept warm in a garage needs none in a frost. The stepped rule's
  «Outside temp» condition becomes «Cabin temp» with the same threshold, once at start; a new
  rule's template is «cabin < 10 °C».

### Fixed
- Charging no longer enters trip data as regen. While the gun is in or the BMS is charging, the
  per-minute trip record stops and the cluster power card's peaks and graphs do not update. In
  trips already recorded, a minute standing still while the battery took more than 3 kW on
  average counts as charging and is left out of the trip page and the forecast: there is no
  regen without moving.
- ADAS shadow mode (4.1.0-dev.17) worked, but its lines never reached the automatic trip log:
  the `AdasShadow` tag was missing from the recorder's filter. Added.
- Trip log: every process that listens to the car echoed each car event as a
  `postEvent device_type: …` line on the `BYDAutoSettingDevice` and `BYDAutoAcDevice` tags, about
  24 thousand lines an hour, three quarters of the log. Those echoes are no longer written; the
  tags' other lines (the stock climate app's seat writes) stay.
