## BYDMate Waze 4.1.0-dev.19 (development pre-release)

### Fixed
- ADAS shadow mode (4.1.0-dev.17) worked, but its lines never reached the automatic trip log:
  the `AdasShadow` tag was missing from the recorder's filter. Added.
- Trip log: every process that listens to the car echoed each car event as a
  `postEvent device_type: …` line on the `BYDAutoSettingDevice` and `BYDAutoAcDevice` tags, about
  24 thousand lines an hour, three quarters of the log. Those echoes are no longer written; the
  tags' other lines (the stock climate app's seat writes) stay.
