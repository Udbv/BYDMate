## BYDMate Waze 4.2.0-dev.17 (development pre-release)

### Fixed
- The ADAS view on the multimedia froze on 3.11 (#23). It showed the car parked, with no lanes and
  no traffic around it, until the multimedia was restarted.
- The likely cause was stopping and restarting the SOME/IP gateway services too often:
  - the dev.10 limit sender did it on every limit change;
  - the parked test did it at the end.
- What changed:
  - once started, the services stay up until the multimedia restarts, as the factory map's do;
  - the restart on a limit change is gone (the car did not take the new value anyway);
  - at the end, "navigation stopped" and empty frames still go out.
