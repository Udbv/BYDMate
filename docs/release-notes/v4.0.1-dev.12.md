## BYDMate Waze 4.0.1-dev.12 (development pre-release)

### Added
- Trip page: a trip in the list opens a full page instead of the dialog. Next to the map and the
  stats it shows:
  - the real cost of the trip at the price of the energy that was in the battery (the mix of the
    home and DC charges before it), the share of DC energy, and what regen saved;
  - where the energy went: rear and front traction, climate, the 12 V side, and what regen
    returned, in kWh;
  - power by minute: stacked bars (rear, front, climate, other, regen below zero), each minute's
    peak and the speed line; a tap on a bar shows that minute in detail;
  - the trip's extremes: traction and regen peaks, top speed, the costliest minute and the
    climate's share, each with its time.
- Per-minute recording while the car is on (a new `trip_minutes` table, about 60 rows an hour):
  the battery power split into the compressor, the 12 V side of the DC-DC (new
  `ENERGY_LOW_VOLTAGE_SIDE_CURRENT` read) and the motors, the motors split between the axles by
  their currents. Trips recorded before this version open with the map and stats only.
