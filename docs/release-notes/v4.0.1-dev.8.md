## BYDMate Waze 4.0.1-dev.8 (development pre-release)

### Changed
- Sensor recorder: the fast signals of a drive (body accelerations and rotation rates, the four wheel
  speeds, steering angle, pedals, motor speed and torque, battery current, suspension accelerometers)
  are recorded at the car's own rate, up to 50 Hz, instead of two lines a second, so a speed bump or a
  hard brake is no longer cut off. Smooth stretches are compressed: a line is written only where the
  signal bends away from a straight line by more than the signal's noise, so a ramp costs two lines.
  Read the file by joining consecutive lines of a fid with straight lines. Other signals keep the
  limit of two lines a second.
