## BYDMate Waze 4.0.1-dev.8 (development pre-release)

### Changed
- Sensor recorder, detail on demand. The fast signals of a drive (body accelerations and rotation
  rates, the four wheel speeds, steering angle, pedals, motor speed and torque, battery current,
  suspension accelerometers) are taken at the car's own rate, up to 50 Hz, into an 8-second buffer in
  memory. The file gets them in full only around something worth seeing: a hard brake or acceleration,
  a sharp body pitch or roll (a speed bump), the traffic light ahead changing, or the driver pressing
  a volume button as a "here" marker. Such a window covers 8 s before and 3 s after and starts with a
  `# detail` line saying what opened it. Elsewhere these signals are compressed to the points where
  they bend away from a straight line by more than their noise (read the file by joining a fid's
  consecutive lines with straight lines). Other signals keep the limit of two lines a second.

### Fixed
- Lane guidance on the HUD showed lanes Waze did not: the 1-px dividers between Waze's lane arrows
  were counted as lanes (three lanes became five, four became seven or eight), the strip was read
  while it was still sliding in, and lanes whose arrow was not recognised were drawn as "straight".
  Now dividers are dropped, a strip is read only once it has settled, and nothing is drawn unless
  every lane's arrow is recognised. Until the arrow table knows Waze's lane icons the HUD will mostly
  show no lanes rather than wrong ones; the trip log keeps the shapes of the unknown arrows to grow it.
