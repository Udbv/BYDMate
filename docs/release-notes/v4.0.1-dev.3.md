## BYDMate Waze 4.0.1-dev.3 (development pre-release)

Groundwork on the Tang L for keeping the seat massage on (#3), checking the ambient light (#4) and the front/rear motor power split (#8).

### Added
- «Реєстратор датчиків» → «Останній запис датчиків → Надіслати в Telegram»: the newest recording in
  Download goes to the bot even when the switch never stopped it (the car went off mid-run).
- The diagnostic log's seats section reads the front-seat massage signals the Tang L catalog names:
  mode, intensity and config per seat, their 3CE/3D3 hal_only twins and the massage switch state.
  Read-only; BYDMate still sends no massage commands.
- A new «ambient» section in the diagnostic log (#4): the readable ambient-light signals the Tang L
  catalog names (brightness, colour, main switch). The Tang L accepts the switch-on command, but its
  own signal cannot be read back (-10011); these show whether the light actually came on.
- A new «motors» section in the diagnostic log (#8): speed and torque of the front and rear motor, each
  inverter's bus voltage and the motor power. A log taken while driving shows the front/rear split.
- The Tang L (DiLink 150) FID dump in `docs/investigations/tang-l-fid-dump-20261024.txt` and the motor
  data notes in `docs/investigations/tang-l-vehicle-data.md`.
