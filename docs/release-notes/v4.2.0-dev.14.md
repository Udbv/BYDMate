## BYDMate Waze 4.2.0-dev.14 (development pre-release)

### Fixed
- The parked test did not reach the car while "Waze limits to ADAS" was on (#23). Making way for
  the test, the drive-time sender stopped the gateway services, so the car refused 1694 of the
  test's 1696 frames (rc=11; 3.11 20:27). Now the sender leaves the services up when it makes way
  for the test. If frames are refused in the middle of a run, the test starts the services itself
  and carries on.
