## BYDMate Waze 4.2.0-dev.1 (development pre-release)

First test build after the stable 4.1.0.

### Added
- Parked ADAS test (#23, step 2, on the owner's decision): Settings → Diagnostics → «Parked ADAS
  test». For 30 seconds it sends the driver assistance the chosen speed limit (30, 70 or 110)
  and a camera at 300 m in the factory map's own messages (navigation active, TrafficInfo,
  naviActionAndCamera) every 200 ms, and once a second reads what the car reports: the camera's
  sign and the assist's limit. Only in P and standing still: checked before the start and before
  every frame, and the first frame that finds the car otherwise ends the test. However it ends,
  an empty limit, an empty camera and «navigation stopped» go out; the gateway services the
  factory map already offers are neither started nor stopped (only when a frame does not go out
  without them are they started, and then exactly those stopped at the end). The test's course
  is in the trip log (`ADAS parked test …` lines). It never runs on its own.
