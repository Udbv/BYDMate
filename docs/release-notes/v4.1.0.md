## BYDMate Waze 4.1.0

Stable build of 4.1.0-dev.23, tested in the car on 2026-10-31. Everything since the previous
stable release (4.0.1-dev.2), in short; the details are in CHANGELOG.md, 4.0.1-dev.3 … 4.1.0-dev.23.

### Added
- Map page: a route to any point coloured by three range scenarios, and «faster or further» at
  90/110/130 km/h with the 160 kW charging stops each speed needs.
- Trips: per-minute recording with outside temperature, wipers and altitude on every minute and
  track point; trip weather; forecast against actual consumption with elevation.
- Massage kept on to the end of the trip; ambient light switched on in the dark.
- Power card on the instrument cluster with per-axle kW.
- ADAS shadow mode: logs what Waze would offer the driver assistance next to what the car sees,
  sending nothing.
- Upstream AndyShaman/BYDMate 4.0.2, 4.0.3 and 4.1.0.

### Fixed
- Waze lanes on the HUD: arrows read by their shape, the lane strip read even when it covers the
  manoeuvre arrow, the lit arrow of a lane the one Waze lit, no arrow flicker.
- Charging (AC and DC) no longer counted as regeneration in trips.
- Automations cut short by power-off or an app update finish where they stopped; «Winter start»
  goes by the cabin temperature.
- Trip log: repeated system lines filtered out, recording starts within 3 s of power-on.
