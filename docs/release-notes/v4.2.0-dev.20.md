## BYDMate Waze 4.2.0-dev.20 (development pre-release)

### Added
- GNSS jamming/spoofing guard. If the position jumps farther than the car could have
  travelled at its own speed, the fix accuracy or timestamp is unrealistic, the position
  stays frozen while the car is moving, or Waze's distance to a camera/destination jumps by
  kilometres (on 3.11 near Kyiv the camera distance jumped by 28-91 km), BYDMate pauses
  Waze-derived data: speed limits and cameras sent to ADAS (the car falls back to its own
  camera), navigation voice, the cruise advice, and the limit/camera on the HUD and
  instrument cluster (the manoeuvre arrow stays). It resumes after 30 s and 5 consecutive
  plausible fixes. Every episode is written to the trip log (GNSS) without coordinates.
